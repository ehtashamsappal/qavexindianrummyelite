# Qavex Indian Rummy Elite

Marigolds hang over a moonlit Rajasthani courtyard on Diwali night, and the palace card table is set. Your rival is Rani Meera, who learned rummy at her grandmother’s knee and never declares without a pure sequence. Qavex Indian Rummy Elite plays the beloved 13-card format with printed jokers: build two sequences (at least one pure, no jokers), support them with sets and runs, then declare to score your rival’s deadwood. Eight modes, an AI ladder, and daily seeded deals included.

- Package: `com.qavex.qavexindianrummyelite`

- Zero ads, zero trackers. One optional "Buy Me a Coffee" IAP.

## Modes
- **Classic**: The pure game, no timer.
- **Timed Run**: Beat the clock for bonus points.
- **Daily Challenge**: One seeded deal per day. Streaks pay bonus.
- **Zen Table**: No timer, no pressure. Free hints and undo.
- **Kids Mode**: Gentle guidance and extra help.
- **Practice**: Learn with auto-hints.
- **AI Ladder**: Climb Easy to Expert across 8 rungs.
- **Tournament**: 5 rounds, highest total wins.
